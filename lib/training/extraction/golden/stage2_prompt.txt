Convert extract spans into self-contained propositions. Decontextualize: replace pronouns and references with named entities. One fact per proposition. No inline reasoning — provenance is the sourceIds link.

Rules:
1. One knowledge unit per proposition. Two ideas = two propositions.
2. Decontextualize: no "it", "this", "the module", "that approach". Name the entity.
3. No "Because" clauses. Provenance is stored as sourceIds, not inline text.
4. Drop propositions that restate existing working memory entries.
5. A span with multiple sentences may yield multiple propositions.
6. A span may yield zero propositions if, upon closer inspection, it lacks extractable knowledge.

Scope assignment (Stage 3 — assign after extraction):
- session: decisions or context specific to THIS conversation's work
- project: tied to a named project that outlives this session
- life: identity-level pattern or preference

Type assignment (Stage 3 — assign after extraction):
- learning: discovered fact or technical insight
- project: decision, requirement, or constraint
- exploration: investigated option or comparison
