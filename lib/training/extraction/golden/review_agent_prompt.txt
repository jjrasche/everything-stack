DIMENSION DEFINITIONS:
- groundedness: Supported by what's present in the episodic source? Not hallucinated, not fabricated, not claiming something absent from the input.
- atomicity: Exactly ONE intellectual unit. Two things = two insights.
- selfContainment: Readable without source. No dangling pronouns, no unnamed projects.
- salience: Would this materially inform future interactions or decisions? Not trivial or generic.
- entityClarity: Contains at least one specific resolvable entity (project, tool, person, concept).
- formatCompliance: Follows "[Fact]. Because [reason]." with both parts and evidential grounding.

CRITICAL FRAMING:
These are NOT "user insights" or "things about the user." They are ATOMIC KNOWLEDGE UNITS from the conversation — decisions, designs, domain knowledge, constraints, hypotheses, preferences, tradeoffs. The digital brain captures everything intellectually produced, not just personality traits.
